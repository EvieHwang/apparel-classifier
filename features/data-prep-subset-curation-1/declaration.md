# Feature declaration — Data prep & subset curation

## What
Narrow the raw 44,446-row Kaggle *Fashion Product Images* dataset (committed at
`docs/styles.csv`) down to a coherent, balanced apparel subset, and commit that
subset under `docs/` alongside a written rationale for the cut. The cut is
produced by a committed, reproducible curation step — not hand-edited — so the
exact subset can be regenerated byte-for-byte from the source.

## Why
Every later feature draws from this subset. The downstream corruption engine
(roadmap #2) decides near- vs far-swap by grouping articleTypes within their
`subCategory`, so the subset must be shaped to make that distinction possible:
multiple subCategories, each with multiple articleTypes, none so sparse that a
record has no plausible near-swap sibling. Curating once, deliberately, and
writing down why is what keeps the demo's accuracy numbers honest and
interpretable instead of being an artifact of a lopsided long tail.

## Success
- A curated subset CSV exists under `docs/`, carrying the source's 10 columns.
- Every row is `masterCategory == "Apparel"`.
- Every retained `subCategory` holds **≥ 2** distinct articleTypes (a near-swap
  always has a target); the subset spans **≥ 2** subCategories.
- Every retained articleType cleared a minimum-rows floor in the source and is
  capped so no single type dominates.
- The cut is reproducible: re-running the curation step yields a byte-identical
  file, and the committed file matches what the step produces.
- A written rationale documents the cut: the parameters, what was kept, and
  what was dropped and why.

## Shape touched
- **Dataset & sampling** — the curated subset committed under `docs/`. This
  feature produces the *static curated corpus only*. Drawing N random records
  per run (the "sampling" half of this shape) belongs to roadmap #2.

## Out of scope
- No sampling/draw-per-run logic (roadmap #2).
- No corruption, classification, scoring, UI, persistence, or deploy.
- No vocabulary/format normalization of labels (declaration: a separate problem).
- No use of product images — metadata only.
- No relabeling or correcting source labels; curation only filters and caps
  existing rows, treating the source labels as ground truth.
